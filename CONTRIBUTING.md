# Contributing

PP-SPEC-035 is maintained as part of the Proof Protocol specification series.

Contributions may be submitted as issues or pull requests.

Contributions should:

- preserve the framework-agnostic architecture of Proof Protocol;
- distinguish source-framework concepts from Proof Protocol concepts;
- avoid reproducing upstream material beyond what is necessary for interoperability;
- identify sources clearly; and
- preserve the normative meaning of the specification.

By contributing, you represent that you have the right to submit the contribution.
